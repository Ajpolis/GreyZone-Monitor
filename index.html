      How grading works

      Attributed: a government, prosecutor or court has publicly said Russia or its proxies were behind it. Suspected: officials link it to Russia, or decline to rule Russia out, without a formal attribution. Unattributed: deliberate sabotage fitting a pattern officials have linked to Russia, with no official link for this incident. Withdrawn: once treated as suspected sabotage, later ruled out or not confirmed. Kept, with the reason.

      About the data

      Built from open sources: official statements, court records and news reports. Every entry is checked by hand before it is published. Dots sit at the nearest town, not the exact site. No individuals are named, including people charged or convicted.
