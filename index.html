How grading works

Attributed
A government, prosecutor or court has publicly said Russia or its proxies were behind it.
Suspected
Officials link it to Russia, or decline to rule Russia out, without a formal attribution.
Unattributed
Deliberate sabotage fitting a pattern officials have linked to Russia, with no official link for this incident.
Withdrawn
Once treated as suspected sabotage, later ruled out or not confirmed. Kept, with the reason.
